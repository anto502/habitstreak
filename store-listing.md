## Full description (4000 chars max)

Habit Streak is the simplest way to build good habits and break bad ones — one day at a time.

Whether you want to drink more water, read daily, exercise, meditate, or quit a bad habit, Habit Streak helps you stay consistent with a clean, distraction-free design and a streak counter that keeps you coming back every day.

**WHY HABIT STREAK**
• Simple, fast, and clutter-free — check off a habit in one tap
• Daily streak counter keeps you motivated to not break the chain
• Achievement badges reward you at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, and 100 days
• Works fully offline — your data stays on your device
• Daily reminder notifications so you never forget
• Light and dark mode
• No account or sign-up required

**FEATURES**
✓ Unlimited custom habits with emoji icons
✓ Daily streak tracking, per-habit and overall
✓ Achievement badge system
✓ Daily reminder notifications
✓ Dark mode
✓ 100% offline — no internet required to use the app
✓ Clean Material Design interface
✓ Free to use, with optional rewarded ads for bonus content

Habit Streak was built for people who want a simple, no-nonsense way to build routines that last — no complicated setup, no social feed, no clutter. Just you, your habits, and your streak.

Download Habit Streak today and start your streak.
