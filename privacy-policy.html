Habit Streak ("the app") is designed with privacy in mind.

Data we store: All habit names, check-in history, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device using local storage. This data is never uploaded to a server and we do not have access to it.

Advertising: The app displays ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded formats). AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads, in line with Google's own privacy policy and advertising ID settings on your device. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

Notifications: If you enable daily reminders, a local notification is scheduled on your device. No reminder content is sent to or from a server.

Data deletion: You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Third parties: We do not sell or share your habit data with any third party. Advertising partners operate under their own privacy policies.

Contact: For privacy questions, contact the developer at the email listed on the Play Store listing.
